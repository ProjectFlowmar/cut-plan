Privacy Policy

Omar Cut Plan. Last updated September 27, 2026.

Who uses this app

Omar Cut Plan is a personal app. Its only user is its owner. It is not offered to anyone else.

What data it reads

With the owner's consent through Google's sign-in screen, the app has read-only access to the owner's Google Health data in three categories: activity and fitness (such as steps, workouts and calories), health metrics (such as resting heart rate), and sleep. It cannot change or delete any Google Health data.

How the data is used

The data is used only to show the owner his own daily summaries (steps, sleep, resting heart rate and workouts) inside the owner's personal training and meal plan. A private script on the owner's own server reads the data about once an hour and saves a short daily summary to a file in the owner's own Google Drive, where the owner's private app page reads it.

Sharing

The data is not sold, not shared with anyone, not used for advertising, and not used to train any AI model. It is not transferred to anyone except as needed to store it in the owner's own accounts.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Omar Cut Plan's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Keeping and deleting data

Summaries are kept in the owner's Google Drive until the owner deletes them. Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
